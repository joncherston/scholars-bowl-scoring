# Scholars' Bowl Day-of Guide

A printable version is in the app at **scoring.suscholarsbowl.com/guide**.

## Room scorers

1. **Sign in.** In Chrome (your normal profile if you can, not a Guest window), go to **scoring.suscholarsbowl.com/room** and enter the PIN on your room card.
2. **Start the round.** The next game is already selected. Check both teams, tick the 4 players for each (it remembers last round), and click **Start round**.
3. **Each tossup:** click the player who buzzed (or press `1`–`4` for team A, `7`–`0` for team B), then **Correct** (`C`) or **Wrong** (`X`). After a wrong answer the other team gets its turn. If nobody gets it: **Dead tossup** (`D`).
4. **Bonus:** pick the value (10, 20, 25, 30, or Other), the number of parts, tap each part answered correctly, then **Save bonus** (`Enter`).
5. **Mistake?** **Undo** (`U`). Press again to keep undoing.
6. **After tossup 12** a yellow box reminds you to announce the score. Click **Done**.
7. **Time called:** **End round** (`E`) and pick the reason. If the score is tied, start the **tiebreaker**: first correct answer wins.
8. **Submit:** read the final score to the moderator, tick the box, and click **Submit round**. The round then locks. Click **Next game**.

**Prefer the spreadsheet?** At sign-in choose **Start scoring with the spreadsheet**: the same scoresheet as past years, a tab for each round, with the room, round, schools and players filled in. Type the moderator's name, then `10` under the player who answered (`0` for a wrong answer) and the points in **Bonus Points**. Row 26 is the tiebreaker. At the end, click **Submit round**. Kept score in your own Excel file (.xls or .xlsx)? Open that round's tab and click **Import scores**; you match any school or player names that differ. Switch screens any time from **Menu**.

**Watch the top-right corner.**
- "All changes saved": everything is on the server.
- "Offline — N actions waiting": keep scoring and **don't close the window**; it catches up on its own. If the computer must be closed or restarted while offline, first click **Download unsaved results** and take the file (flash drive) to the head scorekeeper.
- Computer died? Sign in on another computer with the same PIN and choose **score on this computer**. It continues from the last saved action.

## Head scorekeeper

1. **Live tab:** each room shows its game, score, and when its computer was last heard from (green under 30 s, amber under 2 min, red: go check).
2. **Approve** each round when it's submitted (button in the grid or on the round's page).
3. **A coach disagrees?** Open the round. Compare the tossup-by-tossup table with the coach's sheet, find the first tossup that differs, and use **Correct…**, **Void…**, or **Add a missing entry**. Every change needs a reason and is logged. **Print audit sheet** to show the coach.
4. **A results file from a flash drive:** Live → **Import a results file**. Actions already saved are skipped.
5. **Needs attention:** red items must be fixed before finalizing. For a tie at an award place, click **Resolve tie**, set the order, and record how it was decided.
6. **Finish:** when every counting round is approved, click **Finalize session**, then Reports → **Awards sheet** for the ceremony.

**A team forfeits** (not present within five minutes, a late school's first round, or a team that went home): open the round, choose the team under **Record forfeit**, and give a reason. The opponent gets the win and neither team scores; it counts as final with no separate approval, and the room's computer shows "Forfeit recorded — nothing to score". If a team withdraws mid-event, the Live tab lists its unplayed games with **Record forfeits for all remaining games**. To undo a forfeit, void it in the round's action history.

**If the system is down:** use the printed blank scoresheets (Reports → Blank paper scoresheets; print them beforehand). When the system is back, the room scorer enters the paper sheet into the scorer page tossup by tossup (or, if the room kept score in an Excel scoresheet, uses **Import scores** on that round's tab), and the head scorekeeper approves it as usual.

**Rules at a glance (defaults):** tossups 10 points; no powers or negs. Bonuses only for the team that answered the tossup, with partial credit per part. Individual scores count tossups only. Tiebreaker points decide the game but never count toward total points or individual scores. A forfeit is a win for the opponent with no points for either team. Non-counter games never count. Standings: rounds won, then total points, then head-to-head. Awards (team and individual) go by division: Private, Public 1A–3A, and Public 4A–7A.
